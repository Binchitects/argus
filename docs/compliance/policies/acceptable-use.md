## Using Arena

1. Arena is for [Company]'s work. [Personal use is / is not allowed.]
2. Your account is yours: do not share your password, your second factor or
   your API keys, and do not use anyone else's.
3. Put into a chat only what you may share with the models it uses. Arena's
   safeguards mask or stop some personal data and secrets, but they are a
   safety net, not permission. [Health information / customer data / ...] goes
   only to [the models or rooms the company names].
4. Check what a model says before you act on it or send it on. You are
   responsible for what you use.
5. Do not try to get around Arena's safeguards, limits or access, and do not
   probe it or other systems through it.
6. Respect others' rights: no unlawful, harassing or discriminatory content,
   nothing you have no right to use.
7. Report what looks wrong (a strange answer with someone else's data, a
   suspicious sign-in, a lost device) at once to [where].

## Your devices and where you work

1. Lock your screen when you leave it; Arena signs you out after an hour idle.
2. Devices you use for [Company]'s work have [encryption, updates, a screen
   lock, endpoint protection].
3. Away from the office, use [the VPN]; do not work on health information in
   public places.
4. Downloads from Arena (exports, files) stay on [Company]'s devices and are
   deleted when no longer needed.
